Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  ·  Version 1.1

RentMS ("RentMS," "we," "our," or "the Platform") provides a rent and property management service for landlords, tenants, and administrators in Ghana. This Privacy Policy explains, in detail, what personal data we collect, why we collect it, the legal basis for doing so, how it is protected, and the rights available to you under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). Please read it alongside our Terms of Service.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Policy applies to all users of RentMS — landlords, tenants, and administrators — across our website, web application, and any related services (collectively, the "Services"). By creating an account or otherwise using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood how your information is handled as described here.

RentMS acts as the data controller for account and platform data described in this Policy, and as a data processor on behalf of landlords for tenant information that landlords input to manage their properties.

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
  • "Plaza" means a property or group of rental units managed by a landlord on RentMS.
  • "Payment Partner" means Paystack, our third-party payment processor.
  • "User," "you" means any landlord, tenant, or administrator with a RentMS account.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number, password (encrypted), and role.
  • Property and lease information: plaza and unit details, lease start/end dates, rent amounts, and lease renewal terms, entered by landlords.
  • Communications: messages sent within a plaza's group chat, maintenance requests, announcements, and support inquiries.
  • Profile information: optional profile photo and contact preferences.

3.2 Information Generated Through Use

  • Payment records: amount, date, payment method (Mobile Money network or card), and a unique transaction reference for every rent payment. RentMS does not collect, view, or store your card number, CVV, or Mobile Money PIN — these are entered directly with Paystack.
  • Invite codes: generated by landlords and used by tenants to join a plaza, along with the timestamp of use.
  • Notifications: records of alerts sent regarding rent due dates, maintenance updates, and lease renewals.

3.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system, collected for security and troubleshooting.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the Platform, used to diagnose issues and improve reliability.

3.4 Information From Third Parties

We receive payment confirmation and status updates from Paystack once a transaction is completed, which we use to update your payment and lease records.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and operate your account and display the correct portal for your role.
  • To process rent payments, reconcile them against leases, and generate PDF receipts.
  • To route messages and maintenance requests between the correct landlord and tenant.
  • To send transactional notifications (payment confirmations, due-date reminders, maintenance status, lease renewal alerts).
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the performance of the Platform.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax and financial record-keeping requirements.

We do not use your personal data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell personal data to any party.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your Personal Data on one or more of the following bases:

Basis Example
Performance of a contract Operating your account and processing rent payments you initiate
Consent Optional profile photo, optional notification preferences
Legal obligation Retaining financial records for tax and audit purposes
Legitimate interest Fraud prevention, platform security, and service improvement

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

RentMS uses a minimal set of cookies and browser storage strictly necessary to keep you logged in and remember your session (for example, your authentication token and last-viewed group chat). We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings, though doing so will log you out.

7. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your Personal Data. We share it only in the following circumstances:

  • Payment Partner: Paystack processes all card and Mobile Money payments (MTN, Telecel, AirtelTigo) on our behalf, under its own privacy and security obligations.
  • Within your tenancy: a landlord can see the information needed to manage their own tenants (contact details, payment history, maintenance requests); a tenant can see their own lease, receipts, and messages.
  • Service providers: our database and hosting providers process data solely to store and run the Platform, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by Ghanaian law, court order, or a valid request from a competent authority.
  • Business transfers: if RentMS were to merge with or be acquired by another entity, data may transfer as part of that transaction, subject to this Policy.

8. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured, access-controlled database infrastructure. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access to production data is restricted to what is necessary for operating and maintaining the Platform. While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We retain account, lease, and payment records for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet financial record-keeping and dispute-resolution needs (typically up to seven years for payment records, consistent with standard financial record-keeping practice). Messages and maintenance records are retained for as long as the related tenancy record exists. You may request earlier deletion of data that is not subject to an active tenancy or legal retention requirement.

10. Your Rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with Ghana's Data Protection Commission if you believe your data has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 15.

11. Children's Privacy

RentMS is intended for use by adults entering into or managing rental agreements. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 18. If we become aware that we have done so, we will take steps to delete that information.

12. Third-Party Links

The Platform may reference or link to third-party services, such as our Payment Partner. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their policies separately.

13. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify affected users and, where required, Ghana's Data Protection Commission, without undue delay and in accordance with applicable law.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as RentMS evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and, where appropriate, communicated to users directly.

15. Contact Us

Questions, requests, or complaints about this Policy or your data can be sent through the contact section of our site.
